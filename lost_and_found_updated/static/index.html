Platform

Built to close the loop

Four things done carefully — nothing more, nothing less.

Multi-modal matching

Each report is read twice — the photo and the written description. Both signals are fused into a single confidence score, so a match survives a bad photo or a vague description.

Vector search

A purpose-built vector index keeps lookups fast and accurate, even as the number of reports grows into the thousands. Powered by Qdrant and CLIP.

Instant claim alerts

When a claim is confirmed, both parties receive each other's contact details by email — automatically. No middleman, no waiting.

Private by design

Contact information is only revealed after a verified match. Everyone's details stay safe until a genuine connection is made.

How it works

From report to reunion

1

Report an item

Add a photo, category, location and contact info — whether you found it or lost it.

2

AI reads image & description

The model encodes the item twice — visually and textually — into two compact signatures.

3

Find potential matches

Similar items are surfaced, ranked by a combined confidence score, and annotated with why they match.

4

Review and claim

Confirm the match that's yours and claim it in one click.

5

Coordinate the return

Both people are emailed each other's details to arrange a safe handover.

6

Mark as returned

Once it's back home, close the loop by marking the item as returned.
